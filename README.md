# Fifteen points determine at least nine ordinary lines

**Bryan Zachary Bu, Karam Gill, Hyunjun Yi**

September 2026.

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23343345](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23343345)

## Abstract

We prove m(15) = 9 for the Sylvester–Gallai ordinary lines problem. Only 7 ≤ m(15) ≤ 9 was known, the lower bound being the Kelly–Moser bound of 1958, and it was the first unknown entry of OEIS A003034. The proof classifies 23,174,731 incidence structures and shows none is realisable over the reals, with every algebraic rejection confirmed by an independent SAT test. One system is realisable over ℚ(√−3) but not over the reals. It follows that the Green–Tao formula holds for every 6 ≤ n ≤ 16 except 13.
